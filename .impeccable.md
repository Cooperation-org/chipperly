# Design context

Condensed from `E:\Chipperly\brand-brief.md` (19 Sept 2026) and `docs/ux-plan.md`.

## Audience
Parents and caregivers of neurodivergent children (often autistic, ages about 3 to 12), plus special-education teachers and therapists who share one care plan. Children use a locked, simplified view; many are non-readers. Many visitors are sensory-sensitive or tired parents.

## Use cases
The app replaces laminated visual supports: picture schedule, chip (token) board, visual timer, first-then card, social stories. The marketing site has to show that clearly and get a waitlist sign-up or an app sign-up.

## Brand personality
Calm, warm, predictable. A good preschool teacher's classroom: soft colours, big clear pictures, nothing loud. Made by a parent, sounds like a parent. Not a game (no streaks, urgency), not clinical, not cute in a way that talks down.

## Hard rules
- Motion 150 to 250 ms for UI; no flashing, no bounce, no busy loops. Celebrations soft and short.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: no motion at all, with a good static version.
- Text contrast 4.5:1; actions on teal `#1F6F78`. Logo colours are decoration only. Ochre `#F7931E` is for earned chips only.
- Alegreya (headings) + Alegreya Sans (body), self-hosted.
